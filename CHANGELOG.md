## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Health bar overlay marking the expected damage of enemy casts and channels on nameplates, target, and focus frames.
- Damage of the player's own casts on the target.
- Bar color (dark red by default) and opacity in the settings, with a free color picker via `Custom...` or `/estidmg color`.
- Health bar lookup for common unit frame and nameplate addons, with an automatic search as fallback.
- Settings page, addon compartment entry, and `/estidmg` command.
